Dhamma-Books — all-books Read Aloud update, 4 November 2026

This ZIP replaces the previous Read Aloud Continue correction ZIP.

UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the extracted files and folders into your Dhamma-Books GitHub
   repository. Replace existing files and preserve scripts/ and tests/.
3. Commit and wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing.
4. Refresh with Ctrl+F5 on a computer, or close and reopen the book on mobile.
5. Start reading, pause, and press Continue / 继续. Also close the controls,
   reopen them, and press Continue. Please confirm audible playback on your device.

CHANGES
All 23 reading pages now use one shared speech pause/Continue controller.
This includes Chinese, English and Pāli reading, both Hanyu Pinyin editions,
and the installed-voice path in Burmese books.
Continue retains the voice, speed and last word boundary reported by the voice.
If a voice supplies no boundaries, the current short chunk may repeat.
Late events from canceled speech cannot advance paused or stopped reading.
Burmese online audio retains its playback time and also handles pausing while
loading or just as an audio segment finishes. Mobile screen-awake handling uses
this same paused state.

No book wording, notes, covers, page layout or catalogue links are changed.
The Workings of Kamma PCED correction already uploaded is retained.
