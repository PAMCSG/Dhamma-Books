Dhamma-Books — Read Aloud Continue correction, 4 November 2026

Upload instructions
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the extracted files to your Dhamma-Books GitHub repository, keeping
   the tests/ folder and replacing the corresponding existing files.
3. Commit and wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing.
4. Refresh with Ctrl+F5 on a computer, or close/reopen the book on mobile.
5. In 觉悟资粮, start reading, press 暂停, then 继续. Also try closing the
   controls and reopening them before pressing 继续.

The correction is in the shared text-block book Read Aloud controller.
Its loader version is updated to 1.1.3 in 觉悟资粮, 止观法要 and
佛陀的十二种恶报. Their text and other controls are unchanged.

Continue restarts the current chunk from the last word boundary supplied by
its voice. A voice without boundary events may repeat the current short
chunk. Stop/restart and late canceled speech events are handled separately.

Validation
The original Continue failure was reproduced with a speech-engine simulation
whose paused speaking flag becomes false and whose native resume does nothing.
Chromium browser checks passed for all three real book pages at 1440px and
360px, including pause/Continue, stale completion/error events, voice/speed,
Chinese/Pāli transitions, close/reopen, stop and restart. English and mixed
English/Pāli partial selections were also checked in 觉悟资粮. These checks
use simulated voices; audible playback on the user's device needs a test.
