WHAT CHANGES
- Correct optative identification for vipateyya, pateyya, gaccheyya,
  agaccheyya (āgaccheyya), bhaveyya, labheyya, passeyya, kareyya,
  suṇeyya and jāneyya.
- kareyya identifies karoti, rather than an unrelated karati entry.
- Exact dictionary entries appear first. Verb analysis and an Inflections
  button follow, then the related base-verb definitions.
- The analysis identifies Optative (Sattamī), third-person singular and
  gives a contextual meaning note in English or Chinese.
- Other regular -eyya analyses require a recognised PCED verb and a
  supported forward optative pattern. Multiple candidates are labelled.
- Exact non-verb and gerund entries retain their readings. Identical
  duplicate source records are displayed once.
- All 39 affected HTML pages have updated script cache versions.

The 1,025-word worksheet remains for the Pāli teacher's review. Its draft
mappings have not been imported into the app.
